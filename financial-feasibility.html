Assumptions stated, never hidden

A feasibility is only as good as its assumptions, so each one is written down with where it came from. We do not promise returns, loan approval or any outcome.

Every substantive output records

  • The assumptions used and their source
  • Scenarios and how sensitive the result is to each assumption
  • The data and documents relied on, with dates
  • The chartered accountant who reviewed it
  • Open questions and missing information

What to bring

Whatever you have is enough to start. We tell you what is missing before any fee is agreed.

  • A short description of the project or business
  • Cost estimates, quotations or past accounts you already have
  • Land, property or asset details where relevant
  • Your timeline and how you plan to finance it
  • Any earlier feasibility, report or lender feedback

How an assignment runs

Scope, coordinate, deliver, with one accountable delivery lead from first call to close.

  1. UnderstandWe listen, ask focused questions and confirm what you need answered or done.
  2. ScopeWe agree the question to answer, the inputs, the fee and what is excluded.
  3. CoordinateWe organise your documents and bring in the right chartered accountant.
  4. ReviewThe named CA checks the analysis and assumptions before release.
  5. DeliverYou receive the report or model, with scoped revisions if needed.
