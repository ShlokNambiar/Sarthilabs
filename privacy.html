Privacy notice

How we handle your information

This notice explains what information sarthilabs.in collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to ask us about it. It covers this website and the enquiry and drawing-upload forms on it.

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

What we collect

WhereWhat
Contact form (“Discuss your requirement”)Your name, the email address or phone number you choose to give, the service you are asking about, your organisation if you add it, and the description of your requirement.
Urban Sanction Desk enquiry formThe details above, plus what you tell us about your project (situation, role, building use, location, planning authority and related answers) and any documents you choose to attach (PDF, JPG, PNG, DWG or DXF).
Drawing upload for a first checkThe drawing file you upload and the results produced from it (measurements, findings and, if you ask for it, a traced drawing). You are not asked for your name or contact details there.
Technical informationWhen your browser sends a form or a drawing, our hosting provider’s request logs record your IP address, the time and the address requested. The service also holds your IP address briefly in memory to limit repeated submissions; it is not saved with your enquiry.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. The drawing page remembers your language choice (English or Marathi) in your own browser only.

Why we use it

  • To read your enquiry, contact you about it and propose the work, fees and deliverables.
  • To run the automated first check on a drawing you upload and show you the results.
  • To keep the forms working and protect them from spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The Sarthi Labs team who handle enquiries.
  • The professionals on your assignment (for example an architect, chartered accountant, lawyer or engineer), only where your enquiry needs them and only what they need.
  • Our service providers, which store or deliver information for us: Render, which hosts the enquiry and drawing service (servers in Singapore); GitHub Pages, which hosts this website; and Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on these pages, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

We share information with others only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Drawings uploaded for a first check and their results are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Enquiries and attached documents are kept while we respond to you and, if you become a client, for the assignment. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.
  • Request logs are kept by our hosting provider for its usual operating period.

Your choices and how to contact us

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do that, send a message through the enquiry form: choose “Not sure”, start your message with “Privacy request” and give the email address or phone number you used before. We reply using the contact details you give.

Please do not send portal passwords, OTPs or identity documents we have not asked for.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date.
