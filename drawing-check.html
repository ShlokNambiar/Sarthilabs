Drawing Check

Upload your building-permission drawing. Sarthi reads it, measures the plot and checks it against the PreDCR drawing rules, so AutoDCR can read it the first time.

This checks how the drawing is drawn (layers, closed outlines, names, units) and measures it. It does not apply UDCPR rules or decide approval. Your planning authority decides each case.
