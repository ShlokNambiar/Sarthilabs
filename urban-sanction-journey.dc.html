Who it is for

Three people hold the same problem.

The owner with a notice You added a floor, a room or a shed, or bought a flat with unclear papers. A municipal notice arrived and you do not know what it means. Achieves: understands the rule, what can be regularised, and what to collect before meeting anyone.
The small builder Your approval is stalled or a plan was queried, and every round of queries costs weeks. Achieves: a complete document checklist and a case file to hand to the architect or the authority.
The architect or liaison You run many files and explain the same rules to every client. Achieves: one structured case per client, with cited rules and a status you can share.
What you achieve

You leave with four things.

The rule that appliesIn plain language, with publisher, page and section.
A readiness scoreWhat is complete, what is missing, before you spend a rupee.
A case fileA case code and private key. Site facts listed with their source.
A next actionAn expert appointment, a quote, or payment, whichever you choose.
