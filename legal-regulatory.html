What to bring

Whatever you have is enough to start. We tell you what is missing before any fee is agreed.

  • A short description of the issue or transaction
  • The agreement, title documents or notices you have
  • Names of the parties and any deadline you are facing
  • Earlier correspondence or opinions, if any

How an assignment runs

Scope, coordinate, deliver, with one accountable delivery lead from first call to close.

  1. UnderstandWe listen, ask focused questions and confirm what you need answered or done.
  2. ScopeWe agree the question, the documents in scope, the fee and what is excluded.
  3. CoordinateWe organise the documents and bring in the right advocate.
  4. ReviewThe named advocate reviews and approves the opinion before release.
  5. DeliverYou receive the opinion or review, with scoped follow-up if needed.
