A record you can rely on

Engineering work is documented as it happens, so the next person on site knows what was done and why. We do not promise outcomes that depend on equipment or site conditions outside our scope.

Every substantive output records

  • The equipment, site and conditions found
  • The standards or manufacturer guidance followed
  • The engineer responsible
  • Work done, test results and open items
  • Missing information and recommended next steps

What to bring

Whatever you have is enough to start. We tell you what is missing before any fee is agreed.

  • A short description of the equipment and the work
  • Make, model and age of the machinery
  • Site location, access and any constraints
  • Drawings, manuals or earlier reports you have
  • Your timeline

How an assignment runs

Scope, coordinate, deliver, with one accountable delivery lead from first call to close.

  1. UnderstandWe listen, ask focused questions and confirm what you need answered or done.
  2. ScopeWe agree the work, the site, the fee and what is excluded.
  3. CoordinateWe bring in the right engineers and coordinate vendors and access.
  4. ReviewThe named engineer checks the work and records before handover.
  5. DeliverYou receive the completed work and its documentation.
